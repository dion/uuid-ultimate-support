Privacy Policy

App: UUID Generator Ultimate
Package: com.uuidtools.generator
Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UUID Generator Ultimate ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to being transparent about what data is collected and why.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored locally on your device

  • UUID history — UUIDs you generate are stored in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.
  • App preferences — Settings and preferences are stored locally using Android DataStore. This data remains on your device.

Information collected by third-party services

The App integrates third-party SDKs that may collect data as described below.

  • Google AdMob (free tier only) — Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and diagnostic information to serve relevant ads. Pro subscribers do not see ads and AdMob does not collect data in that context.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — Used to process and manage one-time purchases. Google Play may collect purchase history and account information necessary to complete and validate purchases.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide and improve the App's features. Specifically:

  • Local UUID history is used to display your generation history within the App.
  • Purchase data is used only to unlock Pro features for verified purchasers.
  • Ad data is used by Google AdMob to serve advertisements (free tier only).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any party other than the service providers listed above.

3. Data Retention

Local data (UUID history and preferences) is retained until you uninstall the App or manually clear it within the App. Data held by third-party services is subject to their own retention policies.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can remove it.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Clear your local UUID history at any time from within the App.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device's Settings to opt out of personalized ads.
  • Contact us to request information about data held by third-party providers.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
